Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. DATA CONTROLLER

Kevin Paladino
Contact email: snapflow@proton.me

2. LOCALLY PROCESSED DATA

The SnapFlow application processes all data (screenshots, notes, settings, basic OCR, text search, and spell check) strictly locally on the user's device. The Data Controller does not operate external servers, nor does it collect, store, or analyze any personal or usage information.

3. PURCHASES AND PAYMENTS

Software distribution and sales are handled via the Microsoft Store. Personal and payment data provided during purchase are processed exclusively by Microsoft as an independent data controller, in accordance with its privacy policy (https://privacy.microsoft.com/).

4. THIRD-PARTY TRANSLATION SERVICES

SnapFlow offers an optional text translation feature. If explicitly enabled by the user, the selected text is transmitted via API directly to Google Translate solely to generate the translation. The Data Controller does not retain or store any copy of the translated text. Data processing by Google is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

5. COOKIES AND TRACKING

The application does not use cookies, third-party SDKs, or any tracking/profiling technologies.

6. USER RIGHTS (GDPR)

Under the GDPR, users have the right to access, rectify, or erase their personal data. Because SnapFlow processes data exclusively on the local device and does not retain any personal data or logs on external servers, the Data Controller holds no user data to provide or delete. Users can exercise full control over their data by managing or deleting it directly on their device, or by uninstalling the application. For any privacy inquiries, you may contact the Data Controller at the email address provided in Section 1.

7. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

This privacy policy may be updated. Changes will be communicated through new software releases or via distribution platform release notes.
