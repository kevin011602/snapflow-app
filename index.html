Everything you need.

A powerful alternative to the default system tool. Built for students, creators, and teams who want to capture and organize their screen seamlessly.

Advanced Capture

  • Multi-Monitor Support: Capture all your screens at once or pick a single monitor - perfect for multi-display setups.
  • Window Capture: Capture your active window or a specific application, even if it is partially covered or hidden.
  • Specific Area Selection: Set fixed coordinates to repeatedly capture the exact same area, or use the built-in magnifier for a quick, one-time custom screenshot.
  • Full Page Capture: Enter a URL to screenshot an entire webpage. Use Auto Mode to instantly capture the page while hiding cookie banners and highly invasive pop-ups. If a site requires a login or CAPTCHA, switch to Manual Mode to open an interactive Chrome window, navigate freely, and snap the screenshot when ready.

Draw, Annotate & Hide

  • Vector Annotations: Add shapes, text, and freehand drawings. Text is fully editable – move, recolor, or delete it anytime. Drawings are smooth and can be erased effortlessly for quick corrections.
  • Quick Numbering: Add numbered steps (1, 2, 3...) with a single click, alongside ready-to-use stamps.
  • Instant Redaction: Quickly pixelate sensitive data like passwords or emails before sharing.

Smart Storage

  • Smart Folders: Attach actual files (like PDFs or documents) directly to your screenshot folders.
  • Temporary Mode: Need a quick screenshot just to copy-paste? SnapFlow automatically deletes temporary snaps when closed.
  • Drag & Drop: Move screenshots between folders with a simple drag. You can even drag them directly into other apps like WhatsApp, Slack, or your browser.
  • Labels & Colors: Group your folders by project and assign custom colors for instant visual recognition.

Pro Tools

  • Markdown Notes: Write formatted notes on any screenshot, ideal for studying or meeting summaries.
  • OCR & Translation: Extract and copy text directly from images. Built-in translation included.
  • Powerful Export: Select multiple annotated screenshots and merge them into a single PDF.

100% Offline. Your data stays yours.

We know how sensitive your screen data can be. Text extraction (OCR) and all advanced features run securely on your local PC. No screenshots are ever sent to unknown servers. Work, annotate, and organize in complete privacy.
